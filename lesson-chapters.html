Chapter 1

Your introduction into Japanese language. This chapter covers situations, phrases, currency, and road signs.

Chapter 2

Chapter 2 starts ramping up. You'll start learning restaurant vocabulary, self-introduction, counting, an introduction to the alphabet, intro to Kanji, and more road signs.

Chapter 3

Chapter 3 focuses on more useful phrases for restaurants and at a station. Simple time expressions are introduced as well as numbers in practical situations.

Chapter 4

Chapter 4's emphsis is on learning even more practical restaurant phrases. This chapter will hold the bread and butter. We also practice paying at a resrautant.

Chapter 5

Chapter 5 is a review chapter. We go over a lot of words and phrases from previous chapters as well as learn new ones. Jeaopardy!

Chapter 6

Chapter 6 goes over phrases when you aren't sure what the speaker said. Lessons are slowing down a bit to emphasize more usage of learned vocabulary and ingrain your current knowledge now.

Chapter 7

Chapter 7 goes more in depth with vocabulary and situations. This chapter emphasizes speaking and remembering what you've learned so far. Katakana is also introduced in this lesson.
